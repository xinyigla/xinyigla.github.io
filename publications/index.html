Fit Together: The Impact of Sports-Oriented Peers on Students' Outcomes

Xinyi Chen

Working Paper 2026

This study explores the causal effects of sports-oriented peers on outcomes of individual student in the classroom. Making use of the randomised class assignments in Taiwanese schools, this study presents the evidence regarding the health externalities of individual sports preference.

Bridging the Social Divide: Teacher Attitudes and the Academic Achievement of Internal Migrant Students

Xinyi Chen

Working Paper 2026

This study investigates the impact of teachers' attitudes towards internal migrant students' academic abilities on their test scores in host schools. By focusing on schools that implement randomised class assignments in China, this study suggests the positive effects of teachers' favourable attitudes.

The Long-Term Effects of Higher Education on Non-Cognitive Skills

Xinyi Chen

Working Paper 2026

This study analyses the long-term effects of higher education on individual's Big Five personality traits, risk aversion, mental health, confidence, life satisfaction, and trust, examining outcomes 10 years after graduation on average. This study is built on the differences in exposure to higher education expansion policy in China and constructs an instrumental variable.
